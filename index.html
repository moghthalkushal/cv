Enterprise Architecture Decision System

Business intent becomes governed, reusable delivery through explicit evidence and decision rights.

Business problem

Fragmented evidence and inconsistent review paths slow investment and create avoidable architecture drift.

EA position

Treat architecture as a governed decision system, not a document-production function.

Key decisions

Connect intent, evidence, disposition, reusable standards and measurable outcomes with named owners.

Governance

Decision records, review gates, exceptions, traceability and outcome feedback.

Enterprise value

Faster decisions, better reuse, clearer risk ownership and stronger investment confidence.

My role · domains

EA operating model, executive decision support, portfolio governance, ADRs and standards.

02 · Featured

Governed Agentic AI Platform

Autonomy can scale only when identity, policy, evidence and operations scale with it.

Business problem

Local agent initiatives can duplicate platform capabilities while obscuring risk, cost and accountability.

EA position

Separate business experience from shared AI services; govern autonomy through common enterprise controls.

Key decisions

Reuse enterprise AI services; distinguish orchestration from durable workflow; centralise model access where appropriate.

Governance

Agent identity, entitlement, privacy, residency, cost, resilience, audit, Responsible AI and production gates.

Enterprise value

Reusable capabilities, traceable outcomes, safer scaling and lower duplication across business domains.

My role · domains

Platform boundaries, capability model, readiness gates, AgentOps, gateways, data and knowledge integration.

AEO / GEO Decision Intelligence

Business problem

Governance

Enterprise value
